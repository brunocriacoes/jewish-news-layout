THE PORTUGUESE JEWISH NEWS

A newspaper of the Jewish community, for the Jewish community.

The Portuguese Jewish News shares news, reportage, opinions and reviews about Jewish life in Portugal, Europe and the world.

Our purpose

We inform, connect and preserve Jewish life with independent journalism. The project operates on a voluntary basis, has no effective beneficiaries and is independent of political and economic power.

We recognise that Jewish institutions, people and communities should not be unnecessarily, dangerously or discourteously exposed online.

EDITORIAL STATUTE

Editorial independence, responsibility and care.

  1. An online multimedia publication with news and analysis about Jewish communities in Portugal, Europe and the world.
  2. A volunteer-led project that respects the legislation applicable to journalistic activity.
  3. A publication guided by care for the safety and dignity of Jewish people and institutions.
