Mekor Haim Choir to perform at noon on September 6, 2026, in the Kadoorie Synagogue for the European Days of Jewish Culture: Jewish Music and Love

The celebrated choir will bring Jewish music and love to Porto's historic Kadoorie Synagogue as part of the European Days of Jewish Culture.

By The Portuguese Jewish News•17 August 2026
Sharef𝕏✉

The Mekor Haim Choir will perform at noon on September 6, 2026, in the Kadoorie Synagogue, as part of the European Days of Jewish Culture programme dedicated to Jewish Music and Love.

Hosted in one of Portugal's most important Jewish landmarks, the concert will offer visitors an opportunity to experience a repertoire shaped by memory, tradition and the many voices of Jewish culture.

The Kadoorie Synagogue, home to the Jewish community of Porto, provides a distinctive setting for the event. Its history and architecture make it a natural place for an afternoon that connects music with the living heritage of Jewish Portugal.

“Jewish music carries memory across generations, creating a shared space for celebration and belonging.”

The European Days of Jewish Culture invite audiences across the continent to encounter Jewish life through art, history and dialogue. This year's theme places music at the heart of that conversation.

Admission information and further details will be available through the Portuguese Jewish News and the Jewish community of Porto.
